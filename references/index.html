I spent about 30 minutes reviewing this app and noticed some things about it that I consider serendipitous given the cybersecurity theme of selected topics for the meeting at which this app was shared. When I am considering installing an app, I keep security concerns in mind. During my relatively quick review of this app, I do see a few things that I consider security concerns. To be clear: I have not seen anything that is a clear/obvious indication to me that this app is malicious; rather I see this some things that make me more hesitant to trust than I am to trust apps apps found in Apple's App Store.

Given we decided to talk in more detail about how to choose a trustworthy password manager in our next meeting, I'll also aim to have more specifics on hand I can share at our next meeting about this app too.
