Digital Explorers SIG

Terms

As in any field/discipline, digital technology has its own nomenclature. Authors of user manuals/help guides and articles pertaining to digital technology often use these terms and acronyms without defining them; the authors assume the reader knows what they mean and their meanings are not always obvious from the context in which they are used. One of my key objectives when helping others understand digital technology is to provide some familiarity with terms/acronyms that are commonly/frequenlty appear in online content (like articles, help guides, and videos) so that if/when you happen to encounter them if/when consuming that kind of online content you are better able to understand what is being described/discussed.

The list of terms/acroynms below are those I introduced/defined the Fall 2025 "Tech Skills 101" course. (There are 135 items in the list.)

In handout materials I prepared for DESIG meetings in the first half of 2026, I tried to consistently include definitions of "geek speak" terms/acryonyms I used in each handout. The idea that I am pursuing with this subsection is to provide you with a "geek speak" dictionary of sorts that you can consult if/when you encounter a term/acronym you are uncertain about. I.e., it seems to me that a consolidated reference for terms/acronyms may be a more useful reference than "sprinkling" the definitions of terms/acryonyms across multiple handouts.

My intended next steps with this subsection are to add definitions for about ten terms, share those with the group and ask for feedback on them, and then decide if/how I proceed based on that feedback.
